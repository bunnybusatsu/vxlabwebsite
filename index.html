Contact

Online, you can reach us at visualexperience@ncsu.edu. We have a lab slack, and you can request membership there.

In person, you can find us in our lab at NC State in Eng. Bldg. 2 room 2241 — you'll have to knock. We meet as a group most Fridays at 4 (a bit spotty in the summer). Every other week we gather in the lab and present research to one another. On the other weeks, we meet casually off campus, with Prof. Watson buying ice cream, coffee or beer.
