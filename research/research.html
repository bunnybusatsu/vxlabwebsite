Research

Games

We are interested in improving gamer performance and experience, both for games themselves, and for other high-performance applications such as drone piloting, training and AR/VR. We have published this work at SIGGRAPH, CHI Play, ACM I3D, Computer Graphics Forum, and more.

Graphics

We are interested in engineering new systems that achieve higher temporal realism (e.g. lower latency) — which is greatly underappreciated. Some of our work in this area has been published as a book, and at SIGGRAPH, ACM CHI, Eurographics, EGSR, and ACM I3D.

Visualization

We study the communicative potential of visualization. We have published this work at IEEE Visualization, IEEE CG&A and ACM Mobile HCI.
